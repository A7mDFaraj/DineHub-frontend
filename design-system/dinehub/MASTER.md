# DineHub master design system

Canonical source: [`../../design.md`](../../design.md).

This file exists for UI/UX Pro Max retrieval. Read the root `design.md` in full before designing or implementing any DineHub surface. Its decisions override generated library recommendations.

## Locked identity

- Concept: the **order signal** connecting customer intent to team action and branch visibility.
- Landing typography: local Thmanyah Sans for Arabic, Latin, numbers, and utility labels. No serif or second family.
- Palette: `#22182A`, `#4B2F51`, `#F2644B`, `#CC4937`, `#47AAA1`, `#CCE8E1`, `#DFD2EB`, `#F7F3ED`, `#FFFDF9`, `#685F6D`, `#201827`, `#19131F`.
- Medium: restrained clay-and-glass 3D with paper surfaces and meaningful connection paths.
- Floor: mobile-first from 320px, 44px targets, WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard focus, reduced motion, and complete UI states.
- Avoid: generic gold luxury, serif display faces, neon, blue-purple AI gradients, excessive glass, emoji icons, fake dashboards, and arbitrary bento grids.
